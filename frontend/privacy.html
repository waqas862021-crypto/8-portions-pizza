Information We Collect

When you use the checkout form on this site, we ask for your name, phone number, and (for delivery orders) your address, so your order can be confirmed with the restaurant. This information is not stored on a server — it is only used to build the order summary you send via WhatsApp or read out over a phone call.

Cookies & Local Storage

Your cart is saved in your browser's local storage so it isn't lost if you reload the page. This stays on your device and isn't sent anywhere until you check out.
