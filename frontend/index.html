Our Story

Pizza, made the original way.

Our way of making pizza is derived from the original style from New York City in the early 1900s: dough that's cold-fermented in the fridge, then baked crispy and thin with soft, tender edges.

We follow a healthy method — daily fresh chicken, organic non-hydrogenated mozzarella, Italian extra virgin olive oil, sea salt for flavor, and canola oil (rich in Omega-3) for frying. Our sauces are all made in-house, fresh, from the finest raw ingredients.
